## Traps for the scraper
- The itch TTRPG on-sale feed lists items at "[0% Off]" (Ea-nasir Must Die, HOW'S MY DRIVING?, THE GAS STATION, DEAD MOON, ADVENTURE!). Ea-nasir is only discounted inside a bundle. Good false-positive tests.
- The itch feed is titled "physical games" but the items are downloads.
- itch end dates are UTC (`priceValidUntil`): 06:59Z is the evening before in US time.
- Bundle "was" prices are each site's own retail value / MSRP, not a shelf price.
