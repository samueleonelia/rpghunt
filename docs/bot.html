RPGHuntBot

RPGHuntBot belongs to RPG Hunt, a free daily list of tabletop RPG deals: sales, bundles, discounted books and free giveaways. Every deal on our site links straight to the shop that sells it. We send shoppers to you; we don't resell anything.

Our bot's name in your logs is:

RPGHuntBot/1.0 (+https://samueleonelia.github.io/rpghunt/bot.html)

What we read

  • Public product data: title, current price, normal price and the product link. Usually that's a shop's public product feed (a Shopify /products.json, a WooCommerce store API, an itch.io feed or a public product API), or the public product page when there is no feed.
  • Nothing behind a login. No carts, no checkout, no customer data, no forms.
  • We never try to get around bot walls, CAPTCHAs or blocks. If a shop blocks us, we leave it alone.

How politely

  • We read your robots.txt first and follow it. If it disallows us, by the name RPGHuntBot or *, we don't fetch those pages.
  • One request at a time per site, at most one every 1.5 seconds.
  • We stop and back off when a site answers "429 Too Many Requests".
  • We read each shop about once a day.

Ask us to stop

Block us in your robots.txt and we'll stop at our next visit:

User-agent: RPGHuntBot
Disallow: /

Or open a request on our project page with your shop's address, and we'll stop reading it and remove it from our list. Use the same page if we read too much, too often, or something looks wrong in your logs.
